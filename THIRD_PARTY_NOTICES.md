# Third-party notices

## ThreeUI — "Kage" landing page

The background scene system in `src/components/scene/engine.ts` adapts techniques and code
from ThreeUI's Kage landing page (https://threeui.com/landing-pages/kage-landing-page),
published as a free Community item. Per ThreeUI's Terms of Use, Community package code is
available under the MIT License.

Adapted: the section-anchor scroll mapping (`measure` / `progressFor`), damping and easing
helpers, the Catmull-Rom camera rig with aspect fitting, intro dolly and pointer parallax,
the rising-mote point shader, drifting haze slabs, the glow texture generator, the bloom
chain and composite shader, and the frame-time resolution governor. No Kage images, models,
fonts or temple scene content are included.
